What it actually measures

iOS does not let any app read WiFi signal strength. Not a decibel figure, not a bar count. Any app showing you dBm is estimating.

WiFi Heatmap measures what actually decides whether a video call survives: round-trip time to your own router, how much that varies, packet loss, and real throughput — recorded at the spot you were standing.

Your room in 3D

The LiDAR scanner builds a model of the room as you point at each wall.

The floor fills in

Walk around and each square lights up as it is covered, so you know when you are done.

Scored against your plan

Tell it what you pay for and every room is judged against that, not against the best corner of your own house.

Where the fix goes

It finds the spot that still has strong signal and reaches the dead zone. Both halves matter.

Your scans stay on your iPhone

The 3D model of your home never leaves the device. There is no account, no login, and nothing to delete from a server because nothing was sent to one.
