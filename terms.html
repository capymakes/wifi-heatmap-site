What the measurements are, and are not

This matters more than the usual boilerplate, so it is worth stating plainly.

iOS does not expose WiFi signal strength to third-party apps. WiFi Heatmap therefore measures network performance — round-trip time, variation, packet loss and throughput — and scores rooms on that. It does not report radio signal strength, and any figure it shows is a measurement of behaviour rather than of the radio itself.

Repeater placement suggestions estimate indoor reach using a fixed assumption. Walls, floors and materials the app cannot see through will change the real result. Treat suggestions as a well-informed starting point, not a guarantee.

The app is a diagnostic tool for consumer use. It is not a certified site-survey instrument and should not be relied on where a formal RF survey is required.
