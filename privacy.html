Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

The short version. Your scans, your 3D room models and your measurements stay on your iPhone. There is no account. We cannot see your home, your network name, or where you live.

What stays on your device

Everything that describes your home. Specifically:

  • The 3D models produced when you scan a room.
  • Every measurement taken during a walk, and where in the room it was taken.
  • Your WiFi network name and the identifier of the access point you are connected to.
  • The answers you gave when you first opened the app, including your home type and the speed you pay for.

This information is written to the app's private storage area on your iPhone. It is not uploaded, and deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

What leaves your device

Speed measurements

To measure throughput, the app downloads a block of test data from Cloudflare's public speed measurement endpoint. This is an ordinary internet request. It reveals your IP address to Cloudflare in the same way visiting any website does. No information about your home, your scans or your network is included.

Feature votes

If you vote on the Community screen, a record is written to Apple's CloudKit public database containing the identifier of the feature you voted for and an anonymous identifier derived from your iCloud account. Nothing else. We use this only to rank what to build next. You can withdraw a vote at any time by tapping it again.

Emails you choose to send

If you use the "Suggest something" button, your mail app opens with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and you can edit or delete any part of it first.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. We receive only a confirmation from Apple that a purchase is active on your Apple Account.

Permissions, and why each one is needed

Camera

Used to build the 3D model of your room through the LiDAR scanner. No photographs or video are recorded or stored.

Location

Apple only reveals the name of the WiFi network you are connected to, and which access point you are on, to apps that have location access. That is the only reason the app asks. Your geographic position is never stored, never transmitted, and is not used for any other purpose.

Local network

Used to measure the round trip to your own router, and to list devices that publicly announce themselves on your network. The app reads only the names those devices already broadcast. It does not connect to them, log in to them, or send them anything.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no third-party analytics, no advertising identifiers and no tracking of any kind. It does not build a profile of you and it has nothing to share with anyone, because it collects nothing to share.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Your rights

Because your survey data never leaves your device, the fastest way to erase it is to delete a scan in the app or delete the app itself. To remove feature votes associated with your iCloud identifier, tap the vote again to withdraw it, or contact us and we will remove them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what the app collects, the date at the top of this page will change and the update will be noted in the app's release notes.
