Support

Most problems fall into one of these. If yours does not, email and a person will read it.

The app says my iPhone cannot scan

Room capture uses the LiDAR scanner, which Apple only fits to Pro models — iPhone 12 Pro and later. Support for other iPhones is being worked on and you can vote for it on the Community screen inside the app.

My network name shows as hidden

Apple only reveals the WiFi network name to apps with location access. Grant it in Settings → WiFi Heatmap → Location. Your position is never stored or sent anywhere; it is purely how iOS gates that one piece of information.

Nothing on my network answered

Some office, hotel and guest networks block devices from reaching the router directly. The app falls back to timing against a public address, which still compares rooms accurately, and tells you when it has done so.

Why does it not show dBm like other apps?

Because iOS will not give it to us, or to them. There is no public API for WiFi signal strength on iPhone. Apps displaying a decibel figure are estimating it. We would rather show you four things we genuinely measured.

Restoring a purchase

Settings → Restore purchases inside the app. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they follow you to a new iPhone with no account of ours involved.

Something else

Email kalitaventures@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and roughly what you were doing when it went wrong.
